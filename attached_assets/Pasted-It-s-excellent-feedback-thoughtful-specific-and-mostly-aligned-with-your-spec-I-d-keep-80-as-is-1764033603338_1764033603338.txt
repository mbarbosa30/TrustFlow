What’s strong

	•	Accountability insight: explains why the dilution penalty creates real Sybil costs.
	•	Security critique: calls out vertex-disjoint paths, seed coverage floors, and seed saturation—exactly the right upgrades.

Where I’d tune the feedback
	•	Iteration damping: Suggested per-round decay helps convergence, but be careful—it can bias against legitimate long-path endorsements. Prefer damped recursion with re-normalization (PageRank-style α) and prove a convergence bound (e.g., contractive map or spectral radius < 1).
	•	Outgoing penalty curve: Exponential bite is good, but it may punish bridging nodes (valuable connectors). Consider piecewise: linear up to 15, then steeper; exempt vouchees that later cross an acceptance threshold (retroactive relief).
	•	“ResidualFlow = flow/vouchCount”: good signal of average voucher quality, but specify clipping and add weighted average (e.g., Huber or winsorized) to avoid a single super-strong voucher dominating.
	•	STS components: Don’t drop to 3 too fast. Keep five but learn the weights; the mix guards against single-axis gaming. (Flow+Cut alone can be manipulated.)
	•	KUDOS coupling: I’d keep core strictly neutral. If you ever add a boost, make it opt-in per community and capped, and never influence acceptance—only rank within accepted.

What’s missing (useful additions)
	•	Exact redundancy definition. Publish how “effectiveRedundancy” is computed (direct count, upstream depth weights, edge density). Transparency here prevents confusion.
	•	Anchors & percentiles. Document F95/C95 fallbacks and minimum cohort sizes to avoid anchor volatility in small graphs.
	•	Cut witnesses. Commit to publishing minimal vertex-cut witness sets per accepted user with Merkle proofs; it will make third-party verification cheap.
	•	Seed scoring stability. Add smoothing (exponential moving average) to SeedScore so capacity multipliers don’t whipsaw epochs.

How to de-heuristicize (keep the reviewer’s spirit, make it rigorous)
	1.	Learn weights from data.
	•	Objective: maximize AUC for Sybil detection on labeled/simulated graphs and minimize false-negatives on under-connected legit users.
	•	Method: logistic regression or monotone gradient boosting over {F, C, S, D, PR}, with monotonicity constraints and calibration (Platt/Isotonic).
	•	Output: epoch-pinned weights + reliability diagram.
	2.	Parameter search with sims.
	•	Generate graphs with planted communities + Sybil subgraphs (vary size, degree, collusion).
	•	Stress tests: seed capture, dust-flow, bridge-hub, reciprocal spam, path failure.
	•	Grid/Bayesian-opt over decay λ, dilution curve, acceptance floors.
	•	Report: SNR gain, detection AUC, stability across epochs.
	3.	Convergence & complexity note.
	•	Prove or empirically bound iterative LocalHealth convergence (≤10 iters is fine, but show Δ→0 scaling with |E|).
	•	Adopt α (e.g., 0.85) and show tolerance vs graph size.
