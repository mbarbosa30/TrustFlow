### How Prompts Shape Graph Dynamics and Robustness
Prompts aren't just text—they're the "selection criteria" for edges (vouches), which determine the graph's topology, flow efficiency, and resilience. In the max-flow model, edges are the "capital conduits," so prompts influence:
- **Initial Structure (Seed Phase)**: Early vouches from community seeds (the "founders' fund") set the topology. A prompt like "Vouch for underrepresented contributors with demonstrated impact" creates diverse, high-potential edges (low dilution, strong min-cut to new nodes), while "Vouch for anyone active" floods shallow edges (high dilution, weak residuals).
- **Propagation and Robustness**: 
  - **Robustness**: Good prompts ensure redundant paths (e.g., "require explanation for vouch" = higher cap for thoughtful edges, boosting min-cut). Bad prompts create single-point failures (one weak vouch = low cut_comp, diluting the voucher's score).
  - **Intention's Role**: Prompts encode "quality"—e.g., "Vouch based on potential ROI (e.g., contributions to shared goals)" encourages vouches to high-residual nodes (vouchees with strong incoming), amplifying flow to them and reflecting back as higher local health for the voucher. This "intention multiplier" (e.g., +0.5 to cap for "impact-aligned" vouches) makes the graph "intentional," reducing dilution from non-trustable vouches.
- **Scaling and Efficiency**: As the community grows, prompts "evolve the portfolio"—early ones focus "seed strength" (incoming to seeds), later "scaling" (scaling local health for individual scaling). This catalyzes allocation: High-quality graphs (low dilution, high residuals) attract more KUDOS/mints, self-reinforcing efficient "capital" flow to impactful nodes.

### Implementing Prompts to Shape Robust, Intentional Graphs
To make prompts a "graph architect," integrate as **custom "investment charters"** when creating communities, with mechanics to enforce and evolve them. This ensures intention drives quality (high residuals, low dilution), making the system a catalyst for efficient allocation.

1. **Community Creation: "Thesis Prompt" as Charter**:
   - When creating a community, prompt: "Define your endorsement thesis (e.g., 'Vouch for AI ethics innovators with 2+ contributions'). This shapes vouch caps and scoring."
   - **Implementation**: Store as metadata (e.g., JSON in community contract). Vouches must include a "thesis match" field (e.g., "Why this fits: [text]")—AI/regex scores match (0-1), multiplying cap by (1 + match_score * 0.5) (e.g., perfect match = +50% cap, boosting vouchee residual).
   - **Dynamic Shaping**: Early vouches (first 100) weighted higher (1.5x cap) to "seed" the graph per intention, reducing initial dilution.

2. **Vouch Interface: Prompt-Guided Allocation**:
   - UI: When vouching, show "Thesis Reminder: [community prompt]. Suggested: [AI list of matching nodes at dist=2]. Cost: 10 KUDOS for boost."
   - **Implementation**: 
     - **Cap Multiplier**: Match score (0-1 from prompt alignment) * base cap (1.0 to 1.5x)—e.g., "impact" prompt = higher cap for high-potential vouchees (stronger flow to them, less dilution for you).
     - **ROI Feedback**: After vouch, if vouchee's local health rises >10% in 30 days, +0.5 to your cut_comp ( "successful investment" bonus)—encourages intentional scaling.
   - **Dilution Tie-In**: If vouch doesn't match prompt, +0.05 to dilution penalty (encourages adherence).

3. **Scaling to Allocation Catalyst**:
  - **Community Funds**: Aggregate local health as "portfolio value"—e.g., $1M pool allocated by sum(local_health ** 0.5) to vouched projects (quadratic for impact).
  - **Individual Scaling**: Your local health scales "personal fund" (KUDOS claims), with prompts guiding "portfolio" (outgoing vouches to high-potential).
  - **Evolution**: Prompts "level up" with usage (e.g., governance votes "thesis updates" every 3 months)—graph adapts to community intention.

This way, prompts aren't passive—they're the "constitution" of the trust economy, ensuring robust, quality graphs by aligning intention with mechanics. It maximizes potential: Communities seed intentional "seeds," individuals scale their "portfolio," the network allocates to high-ROI.
