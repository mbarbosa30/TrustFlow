Awesome—here’s a **microcredit-first** design that matches your ranges (ARS 160k–800k, 6–12 months, ~40% APR) and cleanly adds **supporter subsidies + default backstops**, with **trust effects** tied to borrower and supporter behavior.

# 1) Plain goals

* **Access:** short, clear ARS loans sized by **TrustFlow** eligibility.
* **Subsidy:** supporters can **lower the borrower’s cost** (interest buy-down) and/or **cover late installments** (repay-assist).
* **Accountability:** on-time payments nudge trust **up**; defaults/assists nudge trust **down** (conservatively, with proofs).
* **Simplicity:** one “Credit” tab next to Vouch/Score; one admin JSON policy.

# 3) How supporters help (four simple rails)

Pick one or combine—same UX:

### A) Interest Buy-Down (IBD)

Supporter pledges **X ARS/month** (or USD-yield equivalent) to **reduce borrower’s interest**.

* In app: “This loan’s interest reduced **from 40% → 24%** thanks to sponsors.”
* Implementation: each month, apply `min(interest_due, ibd_pool_share)` as **credit** before charging borrower.

### B) Repay-Assist (RA)

If an installment is **late**, a supporter can **cover it instantly** and earns a **small premium** (e.g., 5–8%) repaid **first** when the borrower resumes.

* In app: “Ana’s cuota 3 is late (ARS xx). Cover now, you’ll receive **xx×1.06** first.”
* If the loan finally defaults, remaining RA claims convert to **first-loss covered by guarantee** (see D) or are written down—your policy choice (below).

### C) Interest Vouchers (IV)

Supporters fund **voucher codes** that wipe **N months of interest** for targeted segments (e.g., first-time borrowers, on-time streaks).

* In app: “Voucher applied: **2 months** of interest waived.”

### D) First-Loss Guarantee (FLG)

Supporters (or a reserve) post a **cap** (e.g., ARS 2M) that covers **first defaults** up to that cap.

* Simple waterfall: **Guarantee → Repay-Assist claims → Pool**.
* Publish daily: remaining guarantee capacity.

> All four rails are **off-chain-ledger + receipts** friendly and can be funded by **USD yield** or direct ARS without changing borrower UX.

# 4) Trust effects (straightforward, conservative)

Use only **verifiable events**; update **at epoch** (no real-time flapping).

**Borrower:**

* **+** On-time installment: `+δ_on` (e.g., +0.3 per paid month, cap per loan)
* **–** Late > 7 days (recovered): `–δ_late` (e.g., –1 once per loan if any 7d+ late)
* **––** Default (≥30/60 days late, your policy): `–Δ_default` (e.g., –8 to –15)

**Supporter (Repay-Assist / Guarantee):**

* **+** Assist covered → borrower fully repaid later: `+δ_assist_success` (e.g., +0.5 per assisted installment)
* **–** Assist covered → loan defaulted: `–δ_assist_loss` (e.g., –0.5 to –1; bounded so risk-taking isn’t punished excessively)
* **+** Guarantee active with no calls → small periodic + (participation credit)

**Seeds (if you want meta-governance):**

* If their region repeatedly spawns defaults with low diversity, slight **seed weight trim** next epoch.

> Keep trust adjustments **small** vs. social-graph signals. Publish the **event→delta** table for transparency.

# 9) Simple monthly engine (how money moves)

On each borrower’s **due date**:

1. **Compute borrower charge:** cuota + late fee (if any).
2. **Apply subsidies in order:**

   * **IBD pool** for that month → reduce interest portion
   * **Voucher** (if month matches) → zero interest this month
   * If **late** and supporter opted to **RA**, an assist may **cover** the cuota now (record a claim = amount×1.06).
3. **If unpaid** after grace → mark **Late**, send reminders; **after 60d** → **Default**:

   * Use **First-Loss Guarantee** (if enabled) to cover remaining principal (up to cap).
   * Mark supporter RA claims: if still outstanding, your policy decides—pay from guarantee or mark **lost** (then apply supporter trust delta).

Everything gets a **receipt** (attestation or signed log).
