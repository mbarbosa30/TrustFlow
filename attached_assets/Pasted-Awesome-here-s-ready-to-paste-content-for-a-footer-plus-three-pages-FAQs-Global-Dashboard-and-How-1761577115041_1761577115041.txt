Page: FAQs

What is TrustFlow?

TrustFlow turns private endorsements into a Sybil-resistant trust score using a max-flow/min-cut algorithm from a small, curated seed set. You receive a portable attestation (JWT/VC) proving your level without revealing your social graph.

What do the endorsement levels mean?
	1.	Human — I believe this is a unique person.
	2.	Know — I personally know this person.
	3.	Trust — I vouch for this person’s behavior.
(They map to different edge strengths in the math.)

Is my social graph public?

No. Endorsements are stored as commitments (hashes with salt). By default, no one can see who endorsed whom. You can mutually reveal a specific connection if both sides consent.

How is my score computed?

We build a flow network from the seed set to all users. Your flow and min-cut (redundancy) are normalized into a Standardized Trust Score (STS) from 0–100. See “How it Works” for formulas.

What is “min-cut” in plain English?

It’s the smallest number of independent endorsements that would need to disappear for you to lose your badge. Bigger = more resilient.

How often do scores update?

Each epoch (e.g., hourly). Every epoch publishes a signed bundle so anyone can reproduce results.

How can I improve my score?
	•	Ask Know/Trust endorsements from people already trusted.
	•	Diversify paths (independent routes from seeds).
	•	Avoid spammy patterns; let endorsements mature (cool-down).

Can I link multiple wallets?

Yes. We bind wallets to a DID so you can rotate keys or add addresses without changing your identity.

Can other apps use my trust?

Yes. Export your Trust Attestation and present it anywhere. Verifiers can check the signature or an EAS record (optional).

Will there be tokens/claims?

Optional. Scores can feed a Merkle distribution (points/tokens/credits) per epoch. This is feature-flagged and can be enabled later.

Who chooses seeds and parameters?

A governed list (multisig/DAO). Changes are published and only take effect after a one-epoch timelock.

How do challenges/slashing work?

If enabled, Trust endorsements may include a tiny bond. Proven Sybil abuse can slash the bond. See “How it Works → Incentives”.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can unlink wallets, delete your optional profile, and request removal of future use. Historical epochs stay reproducible but keep edges private (commitments only).

Page: How It Works (Algorithms & Formulas)

Overview

We convert private endorsements into flow from seeds to users. Your acceptance is binary (≥1 unit of flow or min-cut ≥ 1). Your published score is a Standardized Trust Score (STS) in [0,100], built from flow, redundancy, stability, and proximity. Everything is reproducible per epoch; raw edges stay private.

Endorsement Levels → Edge Strength
	•	Human → weight 0.5
	•	Know → weight 0.8
	•	Trust → weight 1.0
(Weights are policy-controlled and published each epoch.)

Graph Construction (Advogato-style)
	•	Split each user u into u^{-}\rightarrow u^{+} with node capacity c(d) based on BFS distance d from any seed:
e.g., c(0)=800, c(1)=200, c(2)=50, c(\ge 3)=20.
	•	Add u^{-}\rightarrow \text{SINK} with capacity 1 (first unit = acceptance).
	•	An endorsement a \to b becomes a^{+}\rightarrow b^{-} with capacity equal to the level weight.
	•	Connect SOURCE → seed_in with large capacity.
	•	Run Dinic / preflow-push to compute max-flow and min-cut.

Acceptance & Tiers
	•	Accepted: flow to u^{-} ≥ 1 (or min-cut ≥ 1).
	•	Tiers (suggested):
	•	Apprentice: STS ≥ 40
	•	Journeyer: STS ≥ 60 and min-cut ≥ 2
	•	Master: STS ≥ 80 and min-cut ≥ 3 and Stability ≥ 0.8

Standardized Trust Score (STS)

We normalize components so scores are comparable across epochs and network sizes.

Let:
	•	f_i = flow to user i
	•	c_i = min-cut for i
	•	d_i = hop distance from seeds
	•	\Delta_i = worst relative drop removing the most-influential inbound edge (stability proxy)

Per epoch (accepted set only), compute anchors:
	•	F_{95} = 95th percentile of \{f\}
	•	C_{95} = 95th percentile of \{c\} (min 3)

Normalize (bounded, robust):
F_i=\min\!\left(1,\frac{\log(1+f_i)}{\log(1+\max(F_{95},\widetilde F_{95}))}\right),\quad
C_i=\min\!\left(1,\frac{c_i}{\max(3,\max(C_{95},\widetilde C_{95}))}\right),\\
D_i=e^{-\lambda d_i}\ (\lambda\approx0.35),\quad
S_i=1-\min(1,\Delta_i)

Blend:
\boxed{\text{STS}_i=100\cdot\big(0.55\,F_i+0.25\,C_i+0.10\,S_i+0.10\,D_i\big)}

Also publish Percentile Rank within the epoch for quick comparison.

Stability, Diversity & Constraints
	•	Stability: we approximate the single-edge influence with fast local recomputes on the residual graph.
	•	Diversity: the system rewards multiple disjoint regions delivering flow.
	•	Constraint (optional): require at least one Trust edge within the first H hops to prevent “Human-only” rings from farming acceptance.

Privacy
	•	Endorsements stored as commitments:
H(\text{endorser DID} \parallel \text{endorsee DID} \parallel \text{type} \parallel \text{salt}).
	•	Mutual reveal requires both parties’ consent.
	•	Profiles are optional and never used in scoring.

Incentives (optional, off by default)
	•	Stake-to-Trust (capped): tiny bond on Trust endorsements; slashed upon successful Sybil challenge.
	•	Reliability mining: edges that carry stable flow across epochs earn a trickle; spam earns nothing.
	•	Diversity multiplier: rewards (not acceptance) increase when flow is supported by independent regions.

Verifiability

Each epoch publishes:
	•	params.json (policy id, weights, capacity schedule)
	•	seed_root, graph_root (Merkle roots)
	•	scores.jsonl (flow, min-cut, STS) + signature
Anyone can recompute and confirm byte-exact results.

Page: Use Cases (short)

Sybil-Resistant Access

Gate community actions (posting, proposals, grants) by tier (e.g., Journeyer+).

UBI / Claims

Distribute a base floor equally to accepted users and allocate a bonus pool by STS (with diminishing returns and diversity bonuses).

Airdrops & Referrals

Reward humans who are stably connected to trusted regions; penalize single-edge farms.

Collusion-Resistant Moderation

Use min-cut + stability to prioritize reports and throttle brigading.

P2P Markets & Credit

Use STS as a soft reputation input for limits, escrow release, or fee discounts.

Footer: Privacy & Terms (concise drafts)

Privacy (short):
We store endorsement commitments (hashes) and per-epoch scores. We do not store salts or raw edges server-side. Optional profile fields are separate and never affect scoring. You can unlink wallets and delete your profile at any time. Historical epochs remain reproducible but do not reveal edges.

Terms (short):
TrustFlow provides probabilistic signals only. Do not use it as the sole basis for high-stakes decisions. Parameters and seed lists can change with notice and a timelock. By using TrustFlow, you agree to our acceptable-use rules (no harassment, spam, or coordinated manipulation). Violations may lead to exclusion from future epochs.
