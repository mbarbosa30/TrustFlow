Here’s a crisp, seed-operator **Community Dashboard** you can ship now. It surfaces *network health*, *credit performance*, and *governance actions* without exposing private graphs.

# At-a-glance (header KPIs)

* **Accepted users** (today / Δ7d / Δ30d)
* **Trust health index** (composite of min-cut, path diversity, seed coverage; 0–100)
* **On-time repayment rate** (rolling 90d) · **PAR30** · **Default rate**
* **Subsidy runway** (days at current sponsor/ yield pace) · **Guarantee cap remaining**

Tooltips link to “How calculated”.

# Network health (trust)

* **Min-cut distribution** (histogram across accepted users)
* **Vertex-disjoint coverage** (share with ≥2 disjoint seed→user paths)
* **Seed coverage share** (per seed, stacked bar; warns if any seed >40%)
* **Path diversity index** over time (line)
* **Reciprocity heatmap** (vouch A↔B within 24–48h; flags ring behavior)
* **Hub & articulation risk** (top 10 nodes whose removal drops acceptance; drill-down shows *witness*, not raw social edges)

Alerts:

* “Seed S2 >45% coverage two epochs”
* “Reciprocity rings detected (k=5, depth≤2)”

# Seed performance (your seed(s))

* **Outgoing endorsements** (count, Δ) · **Conversion** (endorsed → accepted %)
* **False-positive risk** (endorsed who later defaulted / were slashed)
* **Capacity saturation** (if using Advogato-style node capacity)
* **Impact**: new accepted attributed to your seed’s independent paths

Actions:

* Review **pending/flagged** endorsements
* Revoke/annotate (requires reason; logged)
* Invite new endorsers (pre-seeded copy & link)

# Credit performance (community)

* **Active loans** · **Avg ticket** · **Tenor mix (6/9/12m)**
* **Repayment funnel** (on-time / grace / late / default)
* **Cohorts** (by month: on-time %, PAR30, default %, recovery %)
* **Subsidy impact**

  * Interest buy-down applied (ARS / borrowers)
  * Repay-Assist: covered cuotas, premiums repaid, open claims
  * **Effective APR** distribution (after subsidies)
* **Guarantee usage** (calls YTD; remaining cap)

Drill-downs show *aggregates*; per-loan views hide PII but link to receipts/attestations.

# Funding & runway

* **Supporters** (count, active pledges)
* **USD yield → ARS budget** (daily line; FX noted)
* **Daily allocation vs take-up** (budget used %)
* **Projected runway** at current issuance & default rates

# Governance & policy

* **Current policy id** (eligibility gates, APR, tenors, late/default rules)
* **Change log** (who, what, when; timelock countdown)
* **Seed set** (list, join date, last activity)
* **Proposals**: add/remove seed, adjust caps, pause payouts (with quorum & vote)

# Safety & integrity

* **Circuit breaker state** (community payouts / credit pause)
* **Anomaly feed** (spikes in endorsements, same-IP clusters if available, sudden acceptance surges)
* **Reproducibility** (% of epochs re-computed byte-exact by external verifiers)
* **Transparency hashes** (params.json, scores.jsonl, credit_offers.jsonl, receipts) with copy buttons

# Notifications (configurable)

* Coverage skew >40%
* PAR30 > X% or Default > Y%
* Guarantee cap < threshold days
* Recurring reciprocity ring flagged
* External recompute fails

# Seed-facing actions (primary CTAs)

* **Approve/flag endorsements** (batched)
* **Post seed note** (context/guidance shown on vouch modal)
* **Start proposal** (policy/seed change)
* **Export** CSV/JSON of aggregates (min-cut bins, PAR30, subsidy usage)

# Privacy defaults

* No raw edge lists; show *proof objects* (min-cut witness, path count, seed share) and **receipts** only.
* Per-user drill-down requires explicit consent or admin role and still hides counterparties (except consenting parties).

## Minimal API to power it

* `GET /v1/dashboard/overview?community=:cid`
* `GET /v1/dashboard/trust-health?cid=:cid`
* `GET /v1/dashboard/seed-performance?cid=:cid&seed=:addr`
* `GET /v1/dashboard/credit-metrics?cid=:cid&period=90d`
* `GET /v1/dashboard/funding-runway?cid=:cid`
* `GET /v1/governance/policy?cid=:cid` + `GET /v1/governance/changes?cid=:cid`
* `GET /v1/transparency/hashes?cid=:cid&snapshot=:id`

## UI microcopy (pasteable)

* **Trust health index:** “Composite of redundancy (min-cut), independent paths, and seed balance. 70+ is healthy.”
* **Seed coverage share:** “Share of accepted users whose flow depends ≥30% on this seed.”
* **PAR30:** “Portfolio At Risk >30 days: principal outstanding with ≥30 days delay.”
* **Guarantee cap:** “First-loss coverage remaining; calls reduce this.”

This gives seeds a **command center**: they can see whether trust is *diverse and resilient*, whether credit is *healthy and subsidized*, and take **governance actions**—all without exposing anyone’s private graph.
