# For community members (users)

## What is this?

Your community has a shared **USDC wallet** (“the pot”). Each day, a **small fixed %** of whatever is left in the pot becomes available for members to use.

## How do I get a share?

You earn a share by being **trusted** in the community graph:

* people vouched for you,
* your trust paths are **independent** (not all through one person),
* your trust comes from **at least two different seeds**.

If you meet those checks, you’re “**Accepted**” for the day.

## How is the daily pot split?

Simple: higher trust → slightly bigger share.
We convert your trust score into a **weight**, then split the day’s pot across all accepted members by those weights. There’s also a **small daily limit** per person so it stays fair.

## How do I use it?

Two ways:

* **Claim to my wallet:** Tap **Claim** → USDC arrives in your wallet (no complicated steps).
* **Pay directly:** At a participating shop, scan the QR → we send **USDC straight to the merchant**. (You can still claim to yourself if you prefer.)

## What if the pot is low?

Some donors add **pledges**. That means: when the pot can’t cover a payment, a donor’s wallet **tops up that payment automatically** (up to their daily/tx limits). You don’t need to do anything.

## What about receipts?

Every payment gets a **digital receipt** (public but not personal): amount, who was paid, and a short memo. You’ll see a link right after each claim/payment.

### Quick example

* The pot has **10,000 USDC** left.
* The community spends **0.5% per day** → today’s budget is **50 USDC**.
* Ten accepted members share it by trust weight. If your share is **4.80 USDC** and the per-person daily limit is **5 USDC**, you can claim/pay up to **4.80** today.

# For donors

## How your support works

You can help in two ways:

1. **Fund the pot** (send USDC to the community treasury).
2. **Make a pledge** (no upfront transfer): set **daily cap** and **per-transaction cap**. When a member pays and the pot can’t cover it, your wallet **automatically signs** that payment—only then is USDC moved. You always stay in control.

## Why pledges feel great

* **Just-in-time giving:** your funds move only when a real payment happens.
* **Safety rails:** you choose daily/tx limits, allowed merchants, and an end date.
* **Transparency:** you see receipts for what your pledge covered.

## What we guarantee before any payout

* The member passed **objective trust checks** (two independent paths, diversity of seeds).
* The community’s policy and daily budget are **public and signed** (anyone can verify the math).
* Every payout has a **tamper-proof receipt**.

### Donor pledge example

* You pledge **30 USDC/day** with a **5 USDC per-payment** cap.
* If the pot is short on a 4.50 USDC grocery payment, your pledge covers it (4.50).
* If five such payments happen, you’ll hit your **30 USDC/day** and the pledge pauses until tomorrow.

# Shared FAQs (short)

**Q: Who decides the daily amount?**
A: It’s a **fixed % of what’s left** in the pot (e.g., 0.5%/day). That keeps it sustainable.

**Q: Can people game the system by spamming vouches?**
A: No. Acceptance requires **independent paths** and **diverse seeds**—not just many clicks. We publish proofs so anyone can check.

**Q: What if I don’t use my share today?**
A: It doesn’t auto-save, but it also doesn’t waste money—the pot simply remains for tomorrow.

**Q: Is my personal data exposed?**
A: No. Only the **payment receipt** (amount, payee, memo) is public. Your full social graph is **not** revealed.

**Q: I’m a donor—can I stop anytime?**
A: Yes. You can pause or revoke your pledge instantly. Limits are enforced automatically.
