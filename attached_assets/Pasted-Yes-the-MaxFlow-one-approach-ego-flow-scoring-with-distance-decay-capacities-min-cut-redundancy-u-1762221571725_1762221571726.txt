### Why It's **Fair** (Meritocratic & Inclusive)
- **Equity in Scoring**: Unbiased averaging (all non-seeds, including weak nodes) prevents "echo chamber" inflation—weak edges drag scores, rewarding diverse, redundant paths. Seed uniformity (no weights) avoids centralization, and distance-decay favors local trust over distant spam. For KUDOS, inbound-only boosts (receiving influences your score) reward value accrual without self-gaming.

- **Potential Bias Check**: Seeds could centralize if poorly chosen—mitigate with user-selectable co-seeds or dynamic election (e.g., highest median_cut). Overall, it's fairer than token-only systems (e.g., whale votes in early DAOs).
