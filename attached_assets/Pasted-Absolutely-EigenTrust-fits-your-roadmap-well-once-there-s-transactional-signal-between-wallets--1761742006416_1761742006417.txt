Absolutely—EigenTrust fits your roadmap well once there’s **transactional signal** between wallets. Here’s a tight design for (1) weaving EigenTrust into TrustFlow without weakening your **max-flow/min-cut** gate, and (2) **scoring/ranking seeds** so their quality measurably shapes the graph.

---

# 1) Data model for EigenTrust (from transactions)

**Goal:** convert on-chain/off-chain interactions into *local trust* (c_{ij}) that EigenTrust needs.

* For each pair ((i,j)) keep a rolling outcome score (s_{ij}\in[-1,1]).
  Examples of “success” events: escrow released, delivery confirmed, dispute resolved in favor of j, loan installment repaid. Failures: slashed collateral, dispute lost, chargeback.
* Build **robust** local trust (row-normalized, trimmed)
  [
  \hat{s}*{ij}=\text{winsorize}(s*{ij},p=0.05),\quad
  c_{ij}=\frac{\max(0,\hat{s}*{ij})}{\sum_k \max(0,\hat{s}*{ik})}
  ]
  (No negatives in (c_{ij}); failures reduce mass but don’t create “negative trust” that can be weaponized.)
* Optional Bayesian smoothing: start each (i) with Beta((\alpha,\beta)) prior so sparse users aren’t crushed.

Then compute **EigenTrust** by power iteration (as in Kamvar–Schlosser–Garcia-Molina):
[
t=(1-\alpha),e+\alpha, C^\top t,\quad 0<\alpha<1
]
where (e) is a teleport/prior vector (we’ll load seeds into this), and (C) is the matrix ([c_{ij}]). ([nlp.stanford.edu][1])

# 2) How to combine EigenTrust with your flow model (safe)

**Keep acceptance strictly cut-based.** Don’t let EigenTrust decide who’s “in.” Use it as **bounded shaping** + ranking:

### A) Edge & node multipliers (clipped)

* **Endorser quality multiplier (node-level):**
  (g(i)=0.9+0.2,\tilde t_i\in[0.9,1.1]), where (\tilde t_i) is (t_i) normalized to ([0,1]).
  Apply to **(i^{-}!\to i^{+})** (forwarding capacity) *after* distance caps.
* **Pair quality multiplier (edge-level, optional):**
  (h(i,j)=0.85+0.3,c_{ij}\in[0.85,1.15]) on the vouch edge (i^{+}!\to j^{-}).

**Cap total effect** so EigenTrust can only adjust capacities by ~±15% and **cannot** bypass your gates.

### B) Ranking/score smoothing (post-acceptance)

Blend a small PR-style term (or (t_j)) into STS (≤ **5%** weight) so well-behaved transactors sort a bit higher, but **only among accepted users**.

### C) Confidence & health

Use distributional stats of (t) for:

* **User confidence tweak** (+ small bump when (t_j) is high).
* **GHI** (global health): add `eigenTrustGini` (1−Gini of (t)), `seedLeak` (sum of seed (t)), and `txCoverage` (% accepted with ≥N distinct counterparties).

# 3) Seed scoring/ranking that actually *matters*

Seeds are not static; score them and wire the score into both EigenTrust and the flow network.

### SeedScore (S_s\in[0,1]) (publish per epoch)

Combine transparent components:

* **Predictive validity:** among users influenced by seed (s), fraction that stay accepted **after removing** (s)’s edges (resilience).
* **Downstream quality:** average (t_j) (EigenTrust) of users within H hops primarily fed by (s).
* **Complaint/dispute rate:** penalize if vouchees have high failure/dispute rates.
* **Diversity lift:** how many **communities/regions** (s) reaches (Leiden/Louvain).
* **Centralization penalty:** if (s) carries >X% of total seed outflow, reduce (S_s).

### Where (S_s) plugs in

1. **SOURCE→seed capacity:**
   (cap(\text{SOURCE}\to s^{-}) = cap_0 \cdot (0.7+0.6,S_s))  (∈[0.7,1.3]×cap₀).
2. **EigenTrust teleport vector (e):** distribute prior mass across seeds proportional to (S_s).
3. **Coverage rule:** require seed coverage from **seeds with (S_s\ge \tau)** (e.g., 0.6) to count toward the “≥2 seeds” gate.
4. **STS fairness penalty:** small penalty if a user relies >60% on seeds with (S_s<\tau).

This makes “good” seeds amplify healthy regions and “weak” seeds self-throttle—without subjective moderation. (You still keep your **vertex-disjoint ≥2** and **per-seed minimum share** gates.) For seed-quality telemetry you can cite and adapt the TrustRank idea—expert/seed priors + propagation. ([ilpubs.stanford.edu][2])

# 4) Collusion hardening (with EigenTrust in the loop)

* **Row normalization & capping** in (C) prevents a cartel from minting infinite trust; you can also **clip per-pair contribution** so (c_{ij}\le 0.4).
* **Community diversity in the gate:** keep your rule “≥2 communities contribute ≥0.3 of the 1.0 acceptance unit each.”
* **Vertex-disjoint paths** (Suurballe on node-split graph) to stop “two edges, one hub” redundancy illusions. ([Wikipedia][3])
* **SybilRank as risk input** (not a gate): flag low-rank regions by seeded random walks for moderation/audits. ([USENIX][4])

# 6) Minimal compute loop (snapshot)

1. Build **transaction graph** → compute (C) and EigenTrust (t). ([nlp.stanford.edu][1])
2. Score **seeds** → (S_s).
3. Build **vouch flow graph** with node-split + distance-based caps, multiply caps by **(g(i))** and (optionally) edges by **(h(i,j))**.
4. Run **max-flow**, get **min-cut**, check **vertex-disjoint ≥2**, **seed coverage** with per-seed min share and (S_s) threshold.
5. Compute **STS** (add ≤5% EigenTrust).
6. Publish **epoch bundle** (or “snapshot bundle”) with `eigentrust.json` (hash + params), `seedScores.json`, and cut witnesses.

## Why this works (and stays safe)

* EigenTrust is excellent at **aggregating outcome history** into a global reputation (via power iteration), but on its own it’s not Sybil-proof. Your **cut-based gate** remains the barrier; EigenTrust just **shapes** capacity slightly and **ranks** inside the accepted set. ([nlp.stanford.edu][1])
* Seed scoring ties **future influence** to **past predictive quality** and diversity, similar in spirit to **TrustRank**’s expert-seed propagation, but with measurable feedback. ([ilpubs.stanford.edu][2])
* Vertex-disjoint paths and per-seed minimum shares harden against the classic collusion and bottleneck tricks; Suurballe/Tarjan gives you a linear-time check per sink after Dijkstra runs. ([Wikipedia][3])
* For Sybil hunting at scale, **SybilRank** gives you a cheap risk score to feed into **Confidence/GHI** dashboards without dictating acceptance. ([USENIX][4])
