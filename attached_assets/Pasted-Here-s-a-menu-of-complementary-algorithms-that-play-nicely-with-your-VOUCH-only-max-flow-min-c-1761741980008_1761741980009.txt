Here’s a menu of **complementary algorithms** that play nicely with your VOUCH-only + max-flow/min-cut core. I’ve grouped them by **purpose** and noted exactly how to wire each in (gate vs. ranking vs. health/risk). Keep max-flow/min-cut as the **accept/reject guardrail**; use these as add-ons.

---

## A) Redundancy & cut structure (stronger gates & proofs)

* **Vertex-disjoint paths (Suurballe/Tarjan + node-split).**
  Use to *enforce* ≥2 **vertex**-disjoint seed→user paths (not just edge-disjoint). Gate: **Yes** (acceptance).
* **Articulation points & biconnected components (Tarjan).**
  Flag if a user’s acceptance depends on a single cut-vertex. Gate: **No** (risk flag), Health: **Yes**.
* **Gomory–Hu tree (all-pairs min-cuts).**
  Precompute a cut tree to get every user’s min-cut to seeds fast and publish **cut certificates**. Gate: **Yes** (makes audits cheap).

---

## B) Random-walk & centrality (post-accept ranking + risk)

* **Seed-personalized PageRank / TrustRank.**
  Smooth ranking within accepted set; or add ≤5% weight in STS. Gate: **No**.
* **HITS / SALSA (authority–hub).**
  Distinguish “endorsed by many” (authority) vs. “endorses many” (hub). Useful to down-weight hub-only farms. Risk: **Yes**.
* **EigenTrust (on transactional edges, if/when you have tx history).**
  Reputation propagation from signed outcomes; great for markets. Ranking: **Yes**.

---

## C) Social-graph Sybil defenses (risk scoring)

* **SybilRank (seeded random walks).**
  Scores proximity to the seed set; low scores → likely Sybils. Risk: **Yes** (feed Confidence/GHI), Gate: **No**.
* **SybilInfer (Bayesian community model).**
  Produces posterior “human vs. Sybil” probabilities; heavier, periodic. Risk: **Yes**.
* **SybilLimit / GateKeeper variants.**
  Fast, degree-bounded human region tests; good as a **pre-filter**.

---

## D) Spectral & connectivity health (system-level)

* **Algebraic connectivity (λ₂ of Laplacian).**
  Higher = better global robustness. Health: **Yes** (GHI).
* **Conductance / small-set expansion.**
  Detects tight Sybil clusters and single-seed corridors. Health: **Yes**.
* **Effective resistance / commute time.**
  “Electrical” redundancy from seeds to each user; great sanity check on path diversity. Health: **Yes**, Ranking: **light**.

---

## E) Core/cluster structure (diversity & anti-collusion)

* **k-core / k-truss decomposition.**
  Require accepted users to reach a minimal core (or just penalize low-core users). Gate: **Optional** (soft), Risk: **Yes**.
* **Leiden/Louvain community detection.**
  Enforce seed-**region** diversity: e.g., flow share from ≥2 distinct communities ≥30% each. Gate: **Yes**.

---

## F) Probabilistic & robustness-aware scoring

* **Belief propagation / Beta-reputation (Jøsang).**
  Treat each vouch as a noisy sensor; compute a posterior “legitimacy” (p). Use as a **cap** or **confidence** input, not as gate.
* **Max-min fair flow / concurrent flow LP.**
  Allocate seed capacity fairly across all nodes to reduce hub dominance; use to derive a **fairness penalty** in STS or GHI.

---

## G) Anomaly detection (farm/ring hunting)

* **Egonet features (OddBall):** degree vs. edges-inside, star/ring scores.
* **Local Outlier Factor (LOF)/isolation forest** on structural features.
* **Reverse-PR** (on reversed edges) to catch broadcast-only farms.
  Use for **risk flags** and to auto-review sudden waves.

---

## H) Path selection & reliability

* **Most-reliable paths in probabilistic graphs.**
  If you model vouch reliability (p_e), find two high-probability vertex-disjoint paths (Dijkstra on −log p). Gate: **Optional** for high-stakes uses.

---

## How to layer them (safe defaults)

1. **Gate (hard rules):**
   Max-flow/min-cut **+** ≥2 **vertex-disjoint** paths **+** seed-region diversity (≥2 communities with ≥0.3 share each).

2. **Score (STS 0–100):**
   Keep (Flow, Cut, Stability, Distance). Optional: **+5% PPR** cap, **−** fairness penalty if max seed dominates, **−** articulation reliance penalty.

3. **Confidence (user) & GHI (global):**
   Add **SybilRank**, **λ₂**, **conductance**, **effective resistance** spread, **PR Gini**, **seed saturation**.

4. **Risk flags (ops & UI hints):**
   Articulation reliance, ring/star egonets, reverse-PR spikes, LOF outliers, “two paths share one vertex.”

---

## Quick “what to add next” (minimal code lift)

* Swap edge-disjoint → **vertex-disjoint** paths (Suurballe on node-split graph).
* Compute **seed-PPR** once per snapshot; add ≤5% to STS.
* Add **Tarjan articulation** detection; punish reliance.
* Compute **conductance** & **λ₂** on the accepted subgraph and expose in GHI.
* Build a **Gomory–Hu tree** to speed all min-cuts and publish cut witnesses.

This keeps your **flow/min-cut core** intact (the part that makes it Sybil-resistant), while giving you better redundancy guarantees, smoother ranking, and strong system-health signals.
